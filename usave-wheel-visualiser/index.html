Before you get started

Take a photo of your car in bright, even lighting, with the camera at wheel height. Stand at the front corner so the car is at a slight angle (about 15°) — you'll see the front and the whole side, with both wheels showing.

How to get started

  1. Upload your photo — taken from the front corner of the car works best.
  2. Click Add Wheel, pick a style from the carousel, then hit Apply this style.
  3. Drag and resize the wheel to match an existing wheel in your photo. Click Add Wheel again for each wheel you want covered. You can also drag or scroll/pinch on the photo itself to reposition or zoom it.
  4. Photo taken at an angle? Select a wheel and use the Angle slider to match it. Not happy with the lighting? Use the brightness slider to even it out.
  5. Click Save My Look to download an image of your new wheel setup.
  6. Like what you see? Get in touch with the team to talk specifics.
