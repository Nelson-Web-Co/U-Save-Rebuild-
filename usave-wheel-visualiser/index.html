Before you get started

Take a side-profile photo of your car in bright, even lighting, with the camera at wheel height and lined up with the middle of the wheel — this gives the best result.

How to get started

  1. Upload your photo — a side-on shot of your car works best.
  2. Click Add Wheel, pick a style from the carousel, then hit Apply this style.
  3. Drag and resize the wheel to match an existing wheel in your photo. Click Add Wheel again for each wheel you want covered. You can also drag or scroll/pinch on the photo itself to reposition or zoom it.
  4. Not happy with the lighting in your photo? Use the brightness slider to even it out.
  5. Click Save My Look to download an image of your new wheel setup.
  6. Like what you see? Get in touch with the team to talk specifics.
